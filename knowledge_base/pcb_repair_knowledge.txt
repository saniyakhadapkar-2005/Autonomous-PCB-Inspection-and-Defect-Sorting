PCB DEFECT REPAIR KNOWLEDGE BASE

DEFECT: Solder Ball

Description:
A solder ball is a small spherical deposit of solder found on the PCB surface.
It can occur due to excessive solder paste, reflow conditions, contamination,
or improper soldering.

Severity:
Low to Medium.

Repairability:
High.

Recommended Repair:
Inspect the affected area using magnification.
Remove the unwanted solder ball carefully using appropriate rework tools.
Clean the area using suitable PCB cleaning procedures.
Inspect nearby components and solder joints after removal.

Typical Repair Time:
5 to 15 minutes.

Estimated Prototype Repair Cost:
50 to 150 INR.

Decision Guidance:
Usually repairable if the solder ball has not caused significant electrical
damage or short circuit.


DEFECT: Solder Bridge

Description:
A solder bridge occurs when solder unintentionally connects two conductive
pads, pins, or traces.

Severity:
Medium.

Repairability:
High.

Recommended Repair:
Inspect the shorted pins or pads.
Remove excess solder using solder wick or an appropriate rework tool.
Clean the area and inspect for remaining solder connections.
Perform electrical continuity testing if required.

Typical Repair Time:
5 to 20 minutes.

Estimated Prototype Repair Cost:
100 to 250 INR.

Decision Guidance:
Generally repairable when the affected area is accessible and the PCB
itself is not severely damaged.


DEFECT: Missing Component

Description:
A missing component occurs when a required electronic component is absent
from its intended PCB location.

Severity:
Medium.

Repairability:
High to Medium.

Recommended Repair:
Identify the missing component from the PCB design or bill of materials.
Verify component type, value, package, and orientation.
Install the correct replacement component.
Inspect solder joints after installation and perform functional testing.

Typical Repair Time:
10 to 30 minutes.

Estimated Prototype Repair Cost:
100 to 500 INR.

Decision Guidance:
Usually repairable when the correct replacement component is available
and the PCB pads are intact.


DEFECT: Damaged Component

Description:
A damaged component is an electronic component that shows visible physical
or soldering-related damage.

Severity:
Medium to High.

Repairability:
Medium.

Recommended Repair:
Inspect the component and surrounding PCB area.
Determine whether the component itself or surrounding traces are damaged.
If required, remove the damaged component and replace it with a compatible
component.
Perform electrical and functional testing.

Typical Repair Time:
15 to 45 minutes.

Estimated Prototype Repair Cost:
200 to 800 INR.

Decision Guidance:
Repairability depends on component availability and whether surrounding
PCB traces and pads remain intact.


DEFECT: Damaged Board

Description:
A damaged board refers to major physical damage affecting the PCB structure,
such as severe breakage, burning, large damaged areas, or extensive trace
damage.

Severity:
High to Critical.

Repairability:
Low.

Recommended Repair:
Perform detailed visual and electrical inspection.
Check PCB traces, layers, power rails, and affected components.
If damage is extensive or board integrity is compromised, replacement may
be more practical than repair.

Typical Repair Time:
30 to 180 minutes.

Estimated Prototype Repair Cost:
500 to 3000 INR.

Decision Guidance:
Reject when damage is severe, board integrity is compromised, or estimated
repair cost and time exceed the configured limits.

IMPORTANT:
The repair cost and repair time values in this knowledge base are prototype
estimates for demonstration purposes and are not actual industrial pricing.
